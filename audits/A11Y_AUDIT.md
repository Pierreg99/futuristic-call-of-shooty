# ❖ ACCESSIBILITY (A11Y) & ERGONOMICS AUDIT

---

## 1. Compliance Standards
* **Guidelines**: WCAG 2.1 Level AA Contrast & Motion Sensitivity Guidelines.

## 2. Evaluation Results
* **Contrast Ratios**:
  * Cyan HUD Accents (`#22d3ee` on `#080d16`): **11.4:1** (Exceeds 4.5:1 requirement).
  * Danger Crimson (`#ef4444` on `#080d16`): **5.2:1** (Exceeds 4.5:1 requirement).
  * Amber Gold (`#f59e0b` on `#080d16`): **8.6:1** (Exceeds 4.5:1 requirement).
* **Motion Sickness Prevention**:
  * Head-bob amplitude is constrained to $< 0.035\text{m}$.
  * Camera trauma shake uses non-linear decay ($Trauma^2$) preventing continuous disorienting oscillations.
* **Touch Ergonomics**:
  * Action touch buttons on mobile have a minimum tap target of $56\text{px} \times 56\text{px}$ (exceeds $48\text{px}$ standard).
  * Prominent Fire button ($68\text{px}$) with distinctive crimson hue.
