# ❖ TACTICAL MAPS & COMBAT THEATERS

The prototype includes 3 distinct combat theaters dynamically loadable via the HUD menu without reload.

---

## 1. Theater AFG-7: Solar Flare Tower
* **Environment**: High-altitude desert solar rail installation.
* **Layout**: Vertical multi-tier layout featuring a towering central energy monolith ($14\text{m}$ high), crossfire barricades, and flanking gantry towers.
* **Tactical Focus**: Medium to long-range carbine engagements, cover-to-cover flanking around monolith conduits.

---

## 2. Theater Hangar Alpha: Cargo Depository
* **Environment**: Subterranean industrial logistics base.
* **Layout**: Multi-lane layout partitioned by modular shipping containers ($8\text{m} \times 4\text{m} \times 16\text{m}$) and an elevated gantry catwalk ($40\text{m}$ long).
* **Tactical Focus**: Close-quarters combat (CQC), blind-corner ambushes, and high-ground catwalk control.

---

## 3. Theater Apex Spire: Rooftop Helipad
* **Environment**: Cyberpunk megacity pinnacle.
* **Layout**: Open central helipad platform ($24\text{m} \times 24\text{m}$) framed by four towering perimeter spires ($22\text{m}$ high).
* **Tactical Focus**: Rapid 360-degree situational awareness, suppression against swarming aerial drones, and evasive mobility under open skies.
